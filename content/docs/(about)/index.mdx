## About me

I'm Jasper aka r3ps4J and I'm from the Netherlands. In my free time I like working on FiveM and have been doing so for quite some time now (since 2019). As you can imagine, the amount of abandoned side projects I have is insane. I hold a bachelors degree in Software Engineering and am currently persuing a master's degree in Computer Science. I love working with design patterns, and you'll often see me attempt to transfer some of the techniques I've learned to FiveM/Lua.

If you find my resources helpful and feel like you want to help me out, consider supporting me! It will be massively appreciated as studying is not cheap.
